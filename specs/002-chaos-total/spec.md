# Feature Specification: Chaos Total

Experimental flags: collections=true, traps=true, elimination=true, multipleRacers=true. All flags must also be independently switchable for controlled testing.

Collections: purchase or acquire visible scoring cards, build sets and redeem stars at championship end. Scoring modifiers may treat two colors as equivalent for one prediction. Avoid double-counting a single finishing position.

Traps: place a hidden card on a track space. First crossing triggers immediate traps; delayed traps reveal on first crossing and trigger on the second. Friendly fire is allowed.

Elimination: test removing a racer after a defined hazard threshold. Eliminated racers rank after finishers, in reverse elimination order. Ensure predictions remain meaningful.

Multiple racers: explore three toys per team, with speed, disruption and defense roles. Compare selecting one toy per race with simultaneous team entries. Keep betting central.

Refill: explore one refill marker per player for replenishing a card offer; exact cost and timing remain undecided.

Endgame: optional single-star bonus selected by a six-sided die from six categories (distance, overtakes, setbacks, traps triggered, exact predictions, purchases). Define tie handling before testing.

## Evaluation
Compare fun (1–5), perceived control, fairness, replay intent, preferred mode, duration, elimination frustration and scoring clarity. Alternate mode order to reduce novelty bias. Record active flags and rules version. Do not treat group feedback as independent individual responses.
